About AL-GAMEL

From Egyptian farms to your market

Al-Gamel is an Egyptian agricultural export company specializing in the sourcing, packing, and export of high-quality fresh produce to international markets. We work closely with trusted growers and farming partners to ensure a reliable supply of premium Egyptian fruits and vegetables, including oranges, grapes, mangoes, pomegranates, potatoes, onions, and garlic.

Our business is built on three core values: quality, reliability, and commitment. We focus on understanding our customers' requirements, providing consistent product availability, and ensuring timely deliveries that meet international market standards. At Al-Gamel, we believe that successful partnerships are built on trust, transparency, and long-term cooperation. Our mission is to connect the richness of Egyptian agriculture with global markets, delivering value to our partners and bringing the best of Egypt's fresh produce to the world.

Our Valencia oranges are selected for natural sweetness, bright color, and long shelf life. Strict standards in harvesting, packing, and shipping keep the fruit fresh for international buyers.
