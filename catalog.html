Quality Assurance & Shipping Protocols

🌡️ Pre-Cooling & Cold-Chain

All produce undergoes rapid pre-cooling (forced air or hydro-cooling) immediately following harvest to lock in cellular firmness, Brix sugars, and shelf stability.

📊 Digital Temperature Loggers

Every 40ft High Cube Reefer container is outfitted with dual calibrated USB/NFC temperature loggers to provide continuous temperature graphs from origin to destination.

📑 Official Phytosanitary Export Docs

Comprehensive export documentation provided with every shipment: Official Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, Clean on Board B/L, Invoice, and Packing List.
